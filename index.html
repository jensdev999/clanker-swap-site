How a swap works here

Every quote is checked against both Uniswap versions and the better one wins. On V3 the page checks the 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.3% and 1% fee pools. On v4 it checks the standard pools plus the launchpad hook pools (tick spacing 200, fee set by the hook) that tokens like XFEAR and AGENTCLANKER trade in — those pools pair against native ETH, not WETH, so trade them against ETH here. A token whose pool pairs against something else, or whose hook the quoter can't simulate, will show as no route rather than a made-up price. V3 swaps approve SwapRouter02; v4 swaps approve Permit2 and the Universal Router in two steps. Every transaction is confirmed by you in your wallet; funds move wallet-to-pool, never through this page.
